Our single carport SC4830 (4.8m long x 3m wide) is a flat-roof carport built to protect your assets, whether that’s your car, boat, ute, trailer or caravan. It isn’t limited to those uses: ours have been put to work as outdoor entertainment shelters, verandas, and bike and BBQ shelters too. Every carport is made from strong, sustainable steel and comes with a full, up-to-date engineering PS1, so all weather conditions are catered for and your carport is built to standard.

We stock a range of standard sizes and can also custom-make to suit your site. Carports are supplied as a kitset pack and can be freighted NZ-wide, making them a simple DIY project for the home handyman. Invest in a product ‘Built by New Zealand, for New Zealand’.

  • Fully customisable: length, width, height and post positions.
  • PurlinDek roofing spans up to 6m without supports, for a clean uncluttered look.
  • Posts supplied at 3m (900mm in ground, 2.1m clear height); longer posts available.
  • 100×100 fluted roll-formed 0.75g steel posts: strong, sleek, modern.
  • Fix to existing driveways with Top Surface Galv base plates.
  • Add clearlite panels, as often as every 3rd sheet.
  • 155mm fascia/gutter on all four sides; gutters fully encircle the roof.
  • Only 3 main components: roofing iron, fascia/gutter and posts.
  • Fully engineered with up-to-date PS1 supplied.
  • 25-year durability statement; complete kitset and instruction manual included.
